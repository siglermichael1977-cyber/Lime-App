Privacy Policy — Lime App

Effective Date: August 26, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Lime ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Account Information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile avatar/photo
  • Bio or profile description

Content You Create:

  • Posts, captions, and descriptions
  • Comments on other users' posts
  • Likes and reactions to content
  • Saved or bookmarked content

Communication:

  • Messages and direct interactions with other users
  • Feedback, support requests, or inquiries sent to us

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Device Information:

  • Device type, OS, and version
  • Device identifier (IDFA or Android Advertising ID)
  • Mobile network information

Location Information:

  • Precise location (GPS) — only if you grant permission
  • Approximate location based on IP address

App Usage:

  • Features accessed and time spent in each section
  • Navigation patterns
  • Crash logs and performance data
  • Analytics about how you interact with the App

Camera & Photo Library:

  • Images and videos you choose to upload for your avatar or posts
  • We do NOT store these on our servers unless you explicitly upload them as content

Microphone:

  • Audio only if you record video content to post
  • We do NOT access the microphone unless you initiate recording

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Deliver App features and functionality
  • Display your profile and content to other users
  • Send you notifications about activity (likes, comments, follows)
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Aggregate analytics (non-personal) to understand user behavior

3. Data Sharing

3.1 What We Share

  • Public Content: Your posts, comments, profile name, and avatar are visible to other App users
  • Service Providers: We share minimal data with third-party services that help us operate the App (hosting, analytics, customer support)
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

3.2 What We Don't Share

  • We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties
  • We do not share your email address with other users unless you make it public
  • We do not share your precise location with other users by default

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers (Supabase)
  • We use encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to your account is protected by authentication
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access
  • No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security

5. Your Rights & Choices

5.1 Access & Download

You can access your profile, posts, and content at any time through the App.

5.2 Correction

You can update your profile information directly in the App settings.

5.3 Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data by contacting us. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Public content you've posted may remain visible to others for a brief period during processing.

5.4 Location Services

You can disable location access through your device's Settings → Privacy → Location Services.

5.5 Camera & Photo Library

You can deny camera and photo library permissions through your device's Settings.

5.6 Notifications

You can disable push notifications through App settings or your device Settings.

6. Children & Privacy

Lime is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to us should contact us right away.

7. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion (may take up to 30 days for full removal from backups).
  • Content: Deleted when you remove posts/comments, except in backups (retained up to 90 days).
  • Analytics: Aggregated data is retained indefinitely; identifiable analytics are deleted after 12 months.
  • Device Identifiers: Retained for analytics purposes and deleted after 2 years.

8. Your California & International Privacy Rights

8.1 CCPA (California Residents)

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Delete personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

Contact us to exercise these rights.

8.2 GDPR (European Residents)

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, and delete your personal data
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

9. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance.
